**How a Sheba number relates to the bank account number (the owner's question of 20 Nov 2026).** A Sheba of Iran has 26 characters: «IR», then **2 check digits**, then the **3-digit code of the bank**, then **19 digits that name the account** (its kind and its number, filled with zeros at the left). The 2 check digits are calculated from all the other digits, so that a mistyped digit is found; that is why a made-up number is refused although its length is right. Each bank fills the 19 digits by its own rule, so the system cannot reliably build a Sheba from an account number — the bank gives the Sheba (on its website, its ATM, or the account's papers). What the system can do with a Sheba is check it, and read the bank from its 3-digit code.
